If the pilot BE fails (industry-typical 20–30% for topicals, MR and high-variability drugs), the outsourced route restarts steps 1–2 (≈10–14 more weeks of re-contracting and re-queueing). In-house, it's a re-slot. That's why the saving used below is a P50 of ~4 months, not a ceiling: state it as a range, not a guarantee.
